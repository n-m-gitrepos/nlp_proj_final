Article 1: Anarchism
----------------------------------------
Anarchism is a political philosophy and movement that is sceptical of all
justifications for authority and seeks to abolish the institutions it claims
maintain unnecessary coercion and hierarchy, typically including, though not
always limited to, the state and capitalism. Anarchism advocates for the
replacement of the state with stateless societies and voluntary free
associations. As a historically left-wing movement, anarchism is usually
described as the libertarian wing of the socialist movement. Although many
anarchist schools of thought share libertarian, anti-authoritarian,
anti-statist, and anti-capitalist principles, anarchism can be defined more
specifically by its rejection of coercive authority in the conduct of human
relations.

Article 2: Autism
----------------------------------------
Autism, formally called autism spectrum disorder (ASD) or autism spectrum
condition (ASC), is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by deficits
in social communication and social interaction, and repetitive or restricted
patterns of behaviors, interests, or activities, which can include hypo- and
hypersensitivity to sensory input. Autism is a spectrum disorder, meaning
that it can manifest very differently in each person. For example, some are
nonspeaking, while others have proficient spoken language. Because of this,
there is wide variation in the support needs of people across the autism
spectrum.

Article 3: Albedo
----------------------------------------
Albedo is the fraction of sunlight that is diffusely reflected by a body. It
is measured on a scale from 0 (corresponding to a black body that absorbs
all incident radiation) to 1 (corresponding to a body that reflects all
incident radiation). Surface albedo is defined as the ratio of radiosity J_e
to the irradiance E_e (flux per unit area) received by a surface. The
proportion reflected is not only determined by properties of the surface
itself, but also by the spectral and angular distribution of solar radiation
reaching the Earth's surface.

Article 4: A (musical note)
----------------------------------------
A is the sixth note of the fixed-do solfège. It has a frequency of 440 Hz,
which is used as the standard for musical pitch. The note name A is
associated with the submediant of the C major scale. The A above middle C
is commonly used as a tuning standard for Western music, with the
International Organization for Standardization settling on 440 Hz in 1955.
In scientific pitch notation, middle A is called A4.

Article 7: Ampere
----------------------------------------
The ampere, often shortened to amp, symbol A, is the base unit of electric
current in the International System of Units (SI). One ampere is equal to
one coulomb per second. The ampere is defined by taking the fixed numerical
value of the elementary charge e to be 1.602176634 × 10−19 when expressed
in the unit C, which is equal to A⋅s. The ampere was originally defined as
one tenth of the CGS system electromagnetic unit of current, the abampere.

Article 8: Australian rules football
----------------------------------------
Australian rules football, officially known as Australian football, and
colloquially known as Aussie rules or just football within Australia, is a
contact sport played between two teams of 18 players on an oval field,
often a modified cricket ground. The primary objective of the game is to
score goals by kicking the ball through the two tall goal posts at each
end of the ground. The ball may be passed between players by handpass,
where the ball is held in one hand and punched with the closed fist of the
other hand, or by kicking.

Article 9: Ajax (mythology)
----------------------------------------
In Greek mythology, Ajax or Aias was a Greek hero, the son of king Telamon
of Salamis and half-brother of Teucer. He plays a pivotal role in Homer's
Iliad, and the tragedy Ajax written by Sophocles. In the Iliad, Ajax is
the greatest of all Greek warriors after Achilles. He has undying loyalty
to his fellow Greeks and is particularly devoted to Achilles, whom he
greatly admires. Ajax is known for his great physical strength, his skill
in battle, his reliability, and his integrity.
