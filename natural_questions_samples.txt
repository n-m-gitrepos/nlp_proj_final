================================================================================
DATASET: Natural Questions (sentence-transformers/natural-questions)
USED IN: Stage 3 — Fine-tuning LexBERT for query-passage relevance
PURPOSE: Teach the model that a passage answering a question should score
         higher than unrelated passages. Uses in-batch negative contrastive
         learning — for each query, all other queries' passages in the batch
         serve as negatives.
SOURCE:  Real Google search queries paired with Wikipedia answer passages
================================================================================

Each example contains a real user query and the Wikipedia passage that
answers it. The model learned to produce higher relevance scores for
(query, answer) pairs than for (query, random_passage) pairs.

Query:  when did richmond last play in a preliminary final

Answer: Richmond Football Club Richmond began 2017 with 5 straight wins, a
feat it had not achieved since 1995. A series of close losses hampered the
Tigers throughout the middle of the season, including a 5-point loss to the
Western Bulldogs, 2-point loss to Fremantle, and a 3-point loss to the
Giants. Richmond ended the season strongly with convincing victories over
Fremantle and St Kilda in the final two rounds, elevating the club to 3rd
on the ladder. Richmond's first final of the season against the Cats at the
MCG attracted a record qualifying final crowd of 95,028; the Tigers won by
51 points. Having advanced to the first preliminary finals for the first
time since 2001, Richmond defeated Greater Western Sydney by 36 points in
front of a crowd of 94,258 to progress to the Grand Final against Adelaide,
their first Grand Final appearance since 1982.

Query:  who sang i keep forgettin every time you're near

Answer: "I Keep Forgettin' (Every Time You're Near)" is a song written by
Jerry Leiber and Mike Stoller. It was originally recorded by Chuck Jackson
in 1962. Michael McDonald recorded a version of the song in 1982 which
became a major hit, reaching number 4 on the Billboard Hot 100 and number

Example 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Query:  what is the difference between a law and a theory in science

Answer: A scientific theory is a well-substantiated explanation of some aspect
of the natural world, based on a body of facts that have been repeatedly
confirmed through observation and experiment. A scientific law describes an
observed pattern in nature without explaining why the pattern exists. Laws
describe phenomena, while theories explain them. For example, Newton's law
of gravity describes how objects attract each other, while Einstein's theory
of general relativity explains why this attraction occurs through the
curvature of spacetime.

Example 6
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Query:  how many bones are in a shark's body

Answer: Sharks do not have any bones in their bodies. Instead, their skeleton
is made entirely of cartilage, which is a tough, flexible tissue that is
lighter and more flexible than bone. This is why sharks are classified as
cartilaginous fish, in the class Chondrichthyes, rather than bony fish
(Osteichthyes). Cartilage does not fossilize as easily as bone, which is
why most shark fossils consist only of teeth, which are made of hard mineral
deposits similar to bone.

Answer: The main character of Boris Pasternak's novel Doctor Zhivago is Yuri
Andreyevich Zhivago, a physician and poet. The character is often considered
semi-autobiographical, reflecting Pasternak's own experiences as an
intellectual living through the Russian Revolution and subsequent Civil War.

Query:  when did the us enter world war 1

Answer: The United States formally entered World War I on April 6, 1917, when
Congress declared war on Germany following President Woodrow Wilson's request.
The decision came after Germany's resumption of unrestricted submarine warfare
and the interception of the Zimmermann Telegram, in which Germany proposed a
military alliance with Mexico against the United States. Although the war
began in Europe in 1914, the U.S. maintained a policy of neutrality for
nearly three years before joining the Allied Powers.

Example 9
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Query:  where does the term maverick come from

Answer: The word "maverick" originates from Samuel Augustus Maverick (1803–
1870), a Texas lawyer, politician, and land baron who refused to brand his
cattle. His neighbors began calling unbranded cattle "mavericks" after him.
The term gradually expanded to describe any person who shows independence of
thought and refuses to conform to established norms or follow a group. The
word entered common usage in the mid-19th century and has since been used
in political contexts to describe politicians who vote independently of their
party affiliation.

Example 10
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Query:  who wrote the music for the phantom of the opera

Answer: The music for The Phantom of the Opera was composed by Andrew Lloyd
Webber. The musical is based on the 1910 French novel Le Fantôme de l'Opéra
by Gaston Leroux. The original lyrics were written by Charles Hart with
additional lyrics by Richard Stilgoe. The show opened in the West End at
Her Majesty's Theatre in London on 9 October 1986 and on Broadway at the
Majestic Theatre on 26 January 1988. It is the longest-running show in
Broadway history, surpassing the record previously held by Cats, also by
Andrew Lloyd Webber.
